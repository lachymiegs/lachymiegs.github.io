Solo build Tripoli L3 Flight 2026
Project SA Great
Mach 2 · 24,000 ft In final prep

A complete Mach 2 sounding rocket targeting 24,000 ft, designed and built entirely by me as my Tripoli Level 3 certification vehicle. Every primary structure, recovery assembly, and avionics integration is my own work — the only COTS items are the flight computer and parachutes.

The project required me to develop a recovery system from first principles, addressing specific failure modes I had observed across previous flight vehicles. Structural adequacy was verified through hand calculations, load case definition, and FEA in SolidWorks Simulation.

  • Recovery system designed from first principles — failure mode driven
  • Custom avionics bay: sled design, structural interfaces, dual-deploy
  • All primary structures and fin can personally fabricated
  • Engineering documentation package produced for external technical review

03
Self-taught Composites
Pedal Prix Composite Headrests
Year 12 · 2022 Completed

In Year 12 I needed to fabricate custom composite headrests for our Pedal Prix human-powered vehicle. I had no composites experience and no access to mould-making equipment — so I figured it out from scratch in my garage.

I developed a body-casting approach to capture the required geometry without CNC or formal tooling, then taught myself vacuum bagging through iterative trial and error — working out bag sealing, resin ratios, and layup schedule until the parts were structurally sound and correctly fitted. The parts flew on the vehicle.

This is where my composites background began. The same skills now underpin structural work on vehicles targeting Mach 2 and beyond.

  • Self-taught vacuum bagging process — no prior training or instruction
  • Custom body-casting mould approach under severe resource constraints
  • Iterated layup schedule, resin ratios, and bag sealing through testing
  • Parts fitted and flew on competition vehicle
