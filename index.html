Background

I came to engineering through rocketry — I taught myself motor selection, stability analysis, and composite manufacturing before any of it appeared in my coursework. Within six months of starting university I was Structures Lead of ARES Rocketry. I've been leading the team ever since.

I currently work as a Quality Control Engineer at Hargo Engineering, performing dimensional inspection and first article inspection of precision aerospace components to AS9100 standards. I am also Secretary and Launch Control Officer of the Victorian Rocketry Association — Australia's largest Tripoli prefecture.

I want to work on hypersonic vehicles and launch systems.
