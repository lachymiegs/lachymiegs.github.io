Background

I entered university with a strong existing commitment to engineering, particularly in composites manufacturing and flight vehicle systems, which I had already begun developing independently. Joining ARES Rocketry allowed me to continue that trajectory in a more structured environment, and within six months I became Structures Lead. Since then, my involvement has significantly deepened my technical capability and strengthened my long-standing passion for engineering.

I currently work as a Quality Control Engineer at Hargo Engineering, performing dimensional inspection and FAI of precision aerospace components to AS9100 standards. I also serve as Secretary and Launch Control Officer of the Victorian Rocketry Association — Australia's largest Tripoli prefecture.

Alongside launching my Level 3 later this year, I creating a fin-can for what will be the altitude record for the O3400 rocket motor. This record stands at 75 000 ft, we are projecting 81 000 ft, where I am constructing a fin-can to withstand Mach 3.5 flight regimes.

I want to work on launch vehicles and rocket systems.
