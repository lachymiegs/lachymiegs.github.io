ARES' first student researched and developed hybrid rocket. Nitrous-paraffin M-class motor, ~4.5 kN thrust, targeting 10,000 ft. I lead the 120-member team, set technical direction, and manage external stakeholders.

Program overview

ARES Rocketry is a 120-member student engineering organisation at the University of Melbourne. This is the team's most ambitious project — a fully student-researched-and-developed hybrid rocket, the first of its kind in Australia.

As Team Lead I set technical direction, run formal CDR/PDR-style design reviews, manage delivery timelines across six subteams, and serve as the primary point of contact for university faculty, suppliers, and external stakeholders — all under a $60k annual budget.
