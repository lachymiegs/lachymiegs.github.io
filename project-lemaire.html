Project Lemaire

ARES Rocketry's competition entry at the International Rocket Engineering Competition in Texas, June 2025. Flying on an O5500 solid motor targeting 30,000 ft. I designed and built a large proportion of the fin can and airframe as Structures Lead.

↑ Replace with: best photo of Lemaire — on the rail, at the launch site, or in the workshop

Overview

Project Lemaire was ARES Rocketry's entry in the 30,000 ft COTS category at IREC 2025 — one of the world's largest university rocketry competitions, held at Spaceport America, New Mexico. The vehicle flew on an O5500, one of the highest-impulse commercially available solid rocket motors.

We placed 4th in category and 35th overall. The flight itself didn't go to plan — more on that below — but the 4th in category result reflects the technical quality of the build, which is judged independently of flight performance.
